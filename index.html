Two ways to lose money every service

Both overproducing and underproducing cost real money — Nextday narrows the gap using your own patterns.

Overproduce

Excess prep becomes waste — a direct cost loss, even after accounting for what gets reused into other dishes.

Underproduce

Running out means lost sales and customers who leave unhappy when their favorite item is gone.

The gap is learnable

Your own history, plus context like weather and nearby events, predictably shifts demand — Nextday learns it.
